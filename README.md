## How to Enable GitHub Pages (Free Hosting)
1. Go to your repository: https://github.com/drabushaidsujon-arch/academic-research-support-portfolio
2. Click **Settings** → **Pages**
3. Under "Build and deployment":
   - Source: **Deploy from a branch**
   - Branch: **main** (or master)
   - Folder: **/ (root)**
4. Click **Save**
5. Wait 1-2 minutes — your site will be live at:  
   **https://drabushaidsujon-arch.github.io/academic-research-support-portfolio**
